Where this data comes from

Everything in this app is built from documents the Iowa State Fair publishes, plus OpenStreetMap for building shapes. Nothing is invented, and nothing is fetched at runtime — which is why it keeps working when the cell network doesn’t.

    How accurate are the pins?

    The fair describes vendor locations in words (“Outside NE Corner of VI Bldg”), not coordinates, so each pin is derived and its precision varies. Anything imprecise is labelled approx. location in the app.

    A distance in the app combines this pin error with your phone’s own accuracy. Once you’re closer than the two of them together, it stops quoting a figure and says within about <n> ft instead — at that range the honest answer is “look around”, not “30 ft north-east”.

    What the walking time means

    Lists lead with a walking time because that’s the useful number, but it is not a route. It’s the straight-line distance at a deliberately slow pace, and it knows nothing about buildings, fences, closed gates or the crowd three deep around the Butter Cow. Treat it as “how far away this is, in minutes” rather than an arrival time. The distance underneath it is the figure it was calculated from.

    Known gaps

    • Pins point you to the right building, corner or concourse — not to an exact serving window.
    • The restroom list holds 18 of the roughly 40 on the fair’s map — the ones OpenStreetMap has outlined, plus the buildings the fair’s water map says have restrooms. The fair marks the rest with an icon and no coordinates, and guessing at ~40 icon positions by eye would invent precision. Check inside a nearby building too.
    • The fair’s own vendor list truncates some menus mid-item; those stands are marked in the app and a few items may be missing.
    • The dietary lists are the fair’s, and the fair states they’re informational only — check with the vendor if it matters medically.
    • One stand’s published location is literally “TBD”, so it has no pin.
    • Concert lineups, ticket prices, parking and competition schedules are out of scope.

    Offline copy

    The app keeps a complete copy of itself on this device, which is what lets it work with no signal. This is the version of that copy — it updates on its own next time you open the app with a connection.

    Tap to delete it and download a fresh one. Needs a connection, so don’t do this at the fair unless something is clearly broken.
